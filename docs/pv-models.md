## PV models

The Kiesraad models of processen-verbaal (PVs) and their corrigenda, and whether the PV classifier
(`pv_ocr/utils/pv_classifier.py`) supports them. The models are listed on
<https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/overheid-en-democratie/verkiezingen/verkiezingentoolkit/modellen>, which links to
the Kiesraad publication of each. Revision is the election year of the latest version. TK2025 PVs of O 7, P 1f-1 and
P 22-1 use an older layout.

A bijlage counts as a model of its own. Its pages either come as a separate PDF per stembureau (Na 14-2 Bijlage 1,
Na 31-2 Bijlage 1) or are appended to the PV itself (Na 31-2 Bijlage 2). The briefstembureaus (N 29-1, N 29-2, O 20)
count the votes from abroad for TK and EP elections.

Printed vote counts are generated by the election software (OSV). Since every PV is signed, most published PVs of any
model are scans, with or without an OCR text layer of varying quality.
